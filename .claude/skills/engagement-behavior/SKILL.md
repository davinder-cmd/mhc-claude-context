# Engagement, Behavior Change & Learning Loops

Roster for "will people come back and keep going" — distinct from usability (works?), visual design (looks right?), and product strategy (right thing?).

## How to use this skill

1. **Read the ethics guardrail and evidence pressure-test in `roster.md` before proposing a reward/gamification mechanic** — MHC builds health products, not slot machines, and the 2026 research found gamification's health effect is marginal (and may hurt retention in mental-health apps specifically).
2. Follow `roster.md`'s "How to Use This Reference" section — it routes by scenario: designing a loop, designing rewards, designing a learning path, designing notifications, or justifying/killing a gamification idea.
3. **Amy Bucher** is marked the primary practical reference in this file (⭐) and also has her own narrow skill, `bucher-behavior-design`, for when the task is specifically a health-behavior or rewards mechanic — prefer that skill when the trigger is that specific; use this broader roster when surveying the wider loop-design or learning-mastery space.
4. This file is a companion to `ux-usability`, `visual-brand-craft`, and `product-strategy` — cross-reference rather than duplicate their content when a question spans domains.
