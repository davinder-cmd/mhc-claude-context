# Product Strategy

Roster of product-strategy thinkers — what to build, why, and how to ship it. Distinct from usability ("is this usable?"), visual design ("does this look right?"), and design systems ("is this consistent?") — see the comparison table in `roster.md`.

## How to use this skill

Follow the "How to Use This Reference" section at the bottom of `roster.md` — it routes by scenario: deciding what to build, planning execution, validating ideas, or positioning. For positioning specifically, the roster contains April Dunford but there is also a dedicated narrow skill, `dunford-positioning`, for when the task *is* positioning/competitive framing rather than general strategy — prefer that skill when the trigger is that specific.
