# UX & Usability

Roster of usability/UX thinkers. Nielsen's 10 heuristics, Norman's principles, Krug's three tests, and Walter's emotional hierarchy have already been extracted into the actively-scored instrument at `reference/review/ux-heuristics.md` — this skill is the biography and precedent layer underneath that instrument, not a duplicate of it.

## How to use this skill

1. **Running an actual usability review?** Go to `reference/review/_kit.md` → `ux-heuristics.md` / `ux-laws.md` instead. Those are the scored instruments.
2. **Need an argument or precedent** — why a heuristic matters, who to cite, persona methodology, emotional-design maturity — use `roster.md` and follow its "How to Use This Reference" section.
3. **Actually planning or running research** (a discussion guide, synthesizing real sessions, mining existing user signal) is a *separate* skill, `research-synthesis` — this roster names Spool/Hall/Jarrett as precedent, but the operating playbook and the guardrail against synthetic-user research live in that skill, not here.
4. Content design (microcopy, error-state wording) is a *separate* skill (`content-design`) even though Erika Hall appears in both rosters — she wrote different books for each angle (research methods here, conversational copy there).
