# App Photography Brand Statement

Mobile Health Consumer photography reflects health as an integrated, everyday experience rather than a clinical or corrective one. Imagery should feel calm, modern, and human, portraying real people in natural moments that subtly suggest well-being, balance, and progress.

Photography prioritizes lifestyle contexts over medical settings, using natural light, restrained color, and simple compositions to create a sense of trust and approachability. Subjects are inclusive and authentic, captured in candid, unforced moments that emphasize dignity, normalcy, and quiet optimism.

Visual treatment favors simplicity over complexity, with clean framing and ample negative space that complements the digital interface. Health is implied through environment and behavior, not overt symbols or clinical cues, resulting in a contemporary digital health aesthetic that feels credible, supportive, and consumer-first.

## 1. Subject Matter

**DO**

- Show everyday lifestyle moments that imply well-being
- Focus on calm, real-life activities (movement, rest, focus, connection)

**DON’T**

- Show medical settings, illness, or treatment
- Use explicit health or clinical symbols

---

## 2. People & Representation

**DO**

- Feature real, relatable, inclusive people
- Show confidence, autonomy, and authenticity

**DON’T**

- Use overly polished models or exaggerated expressions
- Portray people as patients or passive recipients of care

---

## 3. Composition

**DO**

- Use medium or wide shots with negative space
- Favor natural, editorial-style framing
- Centered focal point for subject matter for cropping purposes

**DON’T**

- Overuse tight headshots or rigidly centered shots
- Crowd the frame with multiple focal points

---

## 4. Lighting

**DO**

- Use natural or soft, diffused light
- Keep exposure even and approachable

**DON’T**

- Use harsh, dramatic, or high-contrast lighting
- Apply cinematic or overly stylized effects

---

## 5. Color & Treatment

**DO**

- Keep colors neutral, warm, and restrained
- Ensure imagery harmonizes with the UI

**DON’T**

- Use heavy filters or strong color casts
- Let photography overpower the interface

---

## 6. Environment & Styling

**DO**

- Use real, lived-in environments
- Choose neutral, timeless wardrobe

**DON’T**

- Use sterile or artificial spaces
- Include logos, bold patterns, or trend-heavy fashion

---

## 7. Emotional Tone

**DO**

- Convey calm, trust, and quiet optimism
- Keep emotion subtle and natural

**DON’T**

- Use forced positivity or dramatic transformation imagery
- Create urgency or fear-based emotion

---

## 8. Role of Photography

**DO**

- Set emotional tone and reinforce trust
- Support clarity and simplicity

**DON’T**

- Explain features or instructions
- Compete with UI hierarchy
