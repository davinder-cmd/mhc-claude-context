Where do healthy habits live?

Healthy habits don't belong in Pick up where you left off because they don't have a finish line. A program/journey/assessment has a "9 of 28" or "62%" or "1 day left" — a habit just has a streak and a checkmark. The rail's whole grammar is completion progress, and habits don't have any.

Below: 4 places they could live, with verdicts.

Option 1 · Dedicated daily habit strip Recommended

"Today's habits"

A new block sitting between Today (trackers) and the in-progress rail. Built for daily check-ins, streaks, and quick tap-to-complete — its own grammar.

Option 3 · Single persistent stripe For the user with one habit

"Today's habit" stripe

When the user has exactly 1 active habit, show it as a slim stripe above the in-progress rail. Drops to nothing when there are zero, expands to Option 1 when there are 2+.

💧
Drink water · 3 of 8 today
3-day streak — keep it going

Why this works: single-habit users (most users early on) get one bright nudge instead of a half-empty grid. Pairs naturally with Option 1 — same component, expanded.

Option 4 · Inside the in-progress rail No

Habits as a rail card

Same place as programs/challenges. Considered for completeness — fails for the reasons stated up top.

Why not: rail cards promise "X of Y" or "N days left." A habit has neither. The dismiss "×" makes no sense (you'd be dismissing a goal you set). And the focus-matched "On focus" treatment doesn't apply — a habit isn't an installed product, it's a self-imposed rhythm.

Recommendation

Add a new block — "Today's habits" — as Option 1. Place it directly under the Today (trackers) strip and above the in-progress rail. Use Option 3's single-stripe collapse when there's exactly one habit. Suppress entirely when there are zero.

  • Order on home: Greeting → Hero → Today (trackers) → Today's habits → Pick up where you left off → Insights → For You
  • Cap at 3 visible; "See all" overflow link.
  • Sort: not-yet-logged today first, then by longest streak.
  • Tap target: primary action is "+ Log" inline (no drilldown for one-tap habits like water).
  • For sensor-tracked habits (10k steps, sleep ≥7h): no button — they auto-fill from the existing tracker block.

This keeps the in-progress rail honest (only items with completion progress) and gives habits the daily-rhythm component they actually need.
