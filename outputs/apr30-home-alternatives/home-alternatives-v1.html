Home Page — 5 Alternatives

Five architectural directions, each addressing the same four requirements. Desktop wireframes at ~1200px. April 30, 2026.

Personalization
Driven by the wellbeing interests survey — content tagged to the user's stated interests.
One hero image
Single full-width hero per page (John's request). No 5-card carousel.
Client items
A reserved place for employer/client features (health fair, OE, benefits selection).
Ongoing programs
Direct re-entry to in-flight DCPs, journeys, habits — not buried in discovery.
Option 1 Hero + Three Lanes Most conventional. Three named lanes, source of every card is obvious.
