Card Kit v1 — 20 layouts, neutral skin

The twenty card patterns from Batch 01, rebuilt as live components instead of screenshots. Every card is real markup on one shared token set, filled with real MHC content so the layouts are being tested against true string lengths. Neutral on purpose: near-monochrome, one accent, no brand type — so what you're judging is layout and information hierarchy, not treatment.

Accent — one

Weighting

Roughly 88% surface + ink, 9% accent, 3% state colour. Colour is never carrying hierarchy except in 04 and 15, where categorisation is the pattern.

For the Figma pass: the :root block at the top of this file is the only thing that changes when this is re-skinned to the v81 navy / SF Pro system. Radius, spacing, and type steps are all tokenised — map them to the existing variable collection and the twenty layouts come across unchanged. Media placeholders are CSS gradients standing in for photography.

Notes for the Figma pass

  • One component, not twenty. Six devices are doing nearly all the hierarchy work here — an image slot that isn't always an image (05, 04, 15), the eyebrow (12, 18), the ruled footer (03, 11, 20), bottom-aligned chips (14, 15), overlay-instead-of-row for state (09, 10, 12), and the right column (08, 13). That's a small enough vocabulary to build as one card with slots and variants rather than a family of separate components.
  • Three base frames cover all twenty: vertical grid card (~300×auto), horizontal row card (~660×auto), and panel-segment (stat). Everything else is slot content and variant state.
  • Equal-height behaviour is load-bearing in 09, 14, 15, and 19 — the chip row and button row must bottom-align across a set. In Figma that's a fill-container auto-layout with a spacer, not a fixed height.
  • Token mapping. The :root block is the whole contract: 4 ink steps, 4 surfaces, 2 rules, 1 accent + tint, 2 state colours, 5 radii, 6 spacing steps, 6 type steps. Map onto the existing v81 collection and re-skin is mechanical.
  • 17 needs a fixed-height head slot. Because the title sits above the image, a title that wraps to two lines pushes that card's image down and breaks the still-life row the pattern exists to create. Every other card absorbs title wrap harmlessly; this one doesn't. Give the head a min-height of two lines.
  • 11 is the one that strains at 375px — verified, not assumed. Holding the image at 44% of a 343px card leaves ~180px for the title, which wraps to three or four lines and stretches the image column tall. It stays usable, but if this pattern ships it needs a stacked variant under ~480px.
  • Not yet covered: empty, loading, locked, and error states; hover and focus-visible. That's the obvious v2.
