01

The audit's highest-leverage recommendation was “turn enrollment into one guided checklist.” That's already designed — here it is beside the current screen.

After · redesign
A guided checklist: one “do this next,” forward progress, one place to enroll, one earning story.

One place to enroll. The separate Points page is folded in; one earning story, one surface.

03

The four lenses, re-read

How each independent view shifts from the current product to the redesign — reported separately, never blended.

Usability

5 major · 5 minor→Largely cleared

The model fixes (checklist, one action, visible progress, one entry point) resolve the cluster of major findings; what remains is low-severity and copy-level.

Visual design

Framework-default→Native system

From a “bolted-on module” to the MHC system — navy, SF Pro, real hierarchy and spacing. The reskin Kim wants, delivered as part of the work.

Conformance

Couldn't measure→Now measurable

The current product couldn't be checked (not our tokens). The redesign is built on the design system, so token/type/color conformance can actually be verified via the Figma bridge.

Accessibility

Unverified→Designed to AA · verify in build

Built to the floors (contrast tokens, 44px targets, rem sizing, visible focus) — but a prototype isn't a pass. Still needs a real WCAG check in the shipped config. Named, not claimed.

04

What's still open — honestly

The redesign resolves the design problems. What's left isn't polish — it's confirmation, feasibility, and verification, mostly outside the design itself.

Accessibility — a build-time pass

Contrast, focus order, tap-target sizes, and 200% text-zoom all need checking in the real configured build, not just the prototype.

Feasibility with Ren (Tech Lead)

“Add to calendar,” day-before reminders, and the completion animation all depend on the no-JS config layer. Some may need a fallback rather than the ideal.

Content sign-offs

Reward wording (per-pay-period, no dollar amounts in-app), the per-phase messages, and a Phase-5 line still need client / D'Arcy confirmation.

Data confirmations from Peak

Whether Peak sends the reason for a phase (not just the number), structured appointment data, and confirmation that visits are telehealth (→ no directions).

Auditing your own work only means something if you're straight about the edges. The design is in good shape; the remaining risk lives in build feasibility and external confirmations — worth flagging now so nothing stalls in the config window.
