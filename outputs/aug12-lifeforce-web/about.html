About LifeForce

The program

Your health, your credit

LifeForce is your company's medical-credit program with Peak Health. Complete a few health steps each year and earn a credit toward your medical premium — one that grows as you move through the program.

How it works

Step 1Get set upAcknowledge the program, complete lab work, and take your Health Assessment.
Step 2Meet with Peak HealthA short visit with a Peak Health nurse to review your results.
Step 3Earn your creditYour medical credit grows with each phase you reach.
