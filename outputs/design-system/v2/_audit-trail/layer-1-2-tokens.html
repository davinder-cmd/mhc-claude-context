AI surface treatment — what's encoded in the canonical card

  • Visible AI label — "From your AI" pill with a primary-tinted dot. AI is never hidden.
  • Subtle gradient surface — primary.50 wash from top-left fade. The card visibly *leans* AI without screaming it.
  • SMS-style brevity — one observation, one number, one suggested action. Not a data dump.
  • Honesty line — "I'm working from your sleep + flare logs. I don't have your medication data yet." AI says when it doesn't know.
  • Memory control — "Manage what your AI remembers" link is always visible. Memory is exposed, editable, deletable.
  • Reasoning available on demand — "Why this?" link reveals the data + reasoning trail without forcing it.
  • Feedback channel — "Not useful" closes the loop and signals the AI is iterating.
