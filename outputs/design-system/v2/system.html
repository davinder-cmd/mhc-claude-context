The cross-cutting AI pattern, decoded

The "AI Coach in Challenges" pilot demonstrates the architectural rule: AI presence is marked with indigo regardless of where it appears, but the action stays in the parent module's voice. Walk through what each color is doing:

  • Indigo wash + indigo tag — the surface visibly leans AI without screaming it. A user inside Challenges immediately knows: "This isn't a teammate; this is my AI Coach."
  • Fuchsia CTA — the action picks up the parent module. Tapping the button stays inside Challenges. Reduces context-shift confusion.
  • Sand + neutral — the structural tokens carry the brand-level warmth and credibility regardless of which module or AI feature surfaces.
  • Honesty line stays — "I'm working from your team's step logs and your calendar." AI says what it knows.

What's still open after v2

  • D2 verification gates — five gates from the D2 v2 deliverable still pending (render-test, low-light, Isabel review, presentation-scale, stakeholder reaction).
  • Module categorical naming — moss/lavender/rose/fuchsia/indigo/warm-as-clay are all placeholder color-led names. Phase 0 §10 deferred the color-led vs role-led question. Names will refactor when that lands.
  • Indigo at full saturation — needs a designer eye. Indigo.500 (#494F95) is currently fairly saturated. Compare against periwinkle from earlier rounds (which was ruled out as primary because it read too synthetic at saturation). The categorical use here is more forgiving but worth verifying.
  • AI surface treatment further applied — the cross-cutting pattern is demonstrated for AI Insight (home) and Challenge AI Coach. Still need to apply to DCP AI Expert (chat surface inside DCP module) to confirm the pattern holds at the chat-thread layer, not just the card layer.
  • Layer 3 (component tokens) — already in tokens-v1-layer3-components.html. Components reference Layer 2 semantic tokens, so the new indigo.* and fuchsia.* additions automatically work in components without a v2 of Layer 3.
  • Layer 4 (icon system) — not yet built. With 6 categoricals now locked, icon module-tinting can be specified.
  • Layer 5 (illustration palette) — variant grid (one composition × N palette swaps) not yet built.
