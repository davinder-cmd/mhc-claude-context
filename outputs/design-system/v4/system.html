Why border, not wash

Pastel primary.50 wash makes sand surfaces look noisy at scale. A 2px primary.600 border is sharper, more editorial, and works on top of any module hue without color clash.

Layer 4 — Icons (v4 editorial — 3 recipes, Material Symbols Outlined)

Three reusable visual recipes. Reduced from five — solid disc / soft squircle / halo / outlined ring / duotone wedge → glyph in container / outlined ring / naked glyph. Module identity expressed through glyph color (at 500), not container fill. Material Symbols Outlined replaces Rounded — Outlined geometry pairs with the editorial register.

Sizing & fill

Sizes: icon.size.sm = 20px (inline, dense), icon.size.md = 24px (default, navigation, standalone), icon.size.micro = 16px (table cells, tags only — exception use). Never 18px or 22px. Stroked = default / informational / unselected; filled = selected / active / confirmation.

Library recommendation

Material Symbols Outlined (Apache 2.0). Variable font: FILL, weight, grade, opsz axes. Solves filled-vs-stroked from one glyph; switching from Rounded (v1–v3) to Outlined was a one-line CSS change with no asset migration. Phosphor (MIT) is the credible alternative if Material's library breadth isn't required. Lucide / Feather ruled out (no native filled). Tabler ruled out (cool / technical reading conflicts with sand surfaces).

Stakeholder narrative

This is the consolidated v4 design system — the active state of MHC's product visual language as of May 2026. It rests on the R2 sky-teal bridge primary (locked under D2 against three candidates), pairs that with a graphite-on-sand neutral architecture, and applies an editorial discipline to components and icons that strips chromatic noise without sacrificing module identity.

Two carryovers from the 2021 brand guidelines: the spirit of the corporate-blue family (now expressed as a softer R2 sky-teal that reads modern-clinical rather than corporate-stiff), and the expectation that every screen carry credibility. What was left behind: lava orange, zesty / lime green, navy night-sky as primary, and the sharper saturation that read as "tech demo" rather than "health platform."

What v4 specifically asserts: color is an accent, not a coat. Module identity lives at the glyph level (a moss healing icon, a lavender chart icon) rather than in pastel container fills that crowd a page. Selection state is signaled by a 2px primary border, not by a tinted surface. The deep-primary gradient is reserved for buyer-hero / sales-deck moments — never for a default member CTA. The cross-cutting AI register (indigo) is universal: the AI surface always reads indigo, even inside a moss-led DCP screen, so members learn that a single visual cue means "this is AI."

How it positions us in the digital health landscape: alongside Hinge and Sword in the "clinical-modern distinctive" tribe (locked under D1) — credible on a CHRO board slide, warm enough on a member's phone. Distinct from Calm / Headspace lifestyle aesthetics (which actively undercut a B2B sales narrative) and from Personify / Sharecare / Thrive (which sit in the bottom-left of the distinctive ↔ generic axis). The top-right corner — distinctive and clinical-modern — is uncontested in the direct-competitor set, and v4 plants the flag there.
