The muddle

v4 surfaces lived in sand.50 with warm-leaning tints (warm.100, primary.100, moss.100) appearing across cards, AI washes, and gradient mists. Warm (~22°) and primary sky-teal (~200°) at light tints landed in adjacent low-chroma territory — what looked clean in isolation read clay-blue-grey at scale. v5 strips the light-tint surfaces, holds chromatic punctuation to 600–700 jewel stops, and retires the two gradient mists that crossed warm × cool light tints.

Variant grid held from v4 (one composition × N palette swaps — Withings illustration economy). v5 shifts the palette swaps deeper: backgrounds now sit in 200/300 rather than 100/200, and accent shapes hit 600/700. The composition reads with more authority and avoids light-tint mush.

Applied moments

Eight screens stress-testing how far the neutral + jewel discipline can flex. Three anchors (Member · Buyer · AI) carry the muddle-fix at its strictest. Five additional moments push color: two with soft single-hue gradients (Wellbeing check-in, Habits streak), three with light-tint module containers (Biometrics, Challenges leaderboard, Rewards milestone). The rule that holds across all of them: single-hue depth — when a light tint appears, it pairs only with deep stops of the same hue. Foreign-hue mixing at light tints is the muddle vector and stays prohibited.

Stakeholder narrative

v5 is a net-new exploration off the v4 system. It tests a single hypothesis: the v4 application was producing muddled output where warm sand surfaces met blue-leaning light tints (primary.100, moss.100, etc.), and the fix is to retire that light-tint range from product surfaces and shift chromatic identity into the jewel range — categoricals at 600/700, never at 100/200.

What carried forward unchanged: the R2 sky-teal bridge primary at #2A6489 (locked under D2), the neutral-on-sand split, the Clinical-Modern Distinctive tribe (D1), the cross-cutting AI principle (indigo means AI, regardless of host module), the dot-and-caps editorial badges, the variant-grid illustration economy, and the open-source Material Symbols Outlined library.

What v5 changes: light tints retire from product surfaces; the two gradient mists that crossed warm × cool tints are pruned; module-specific deep gradients (gradient.deep-moss, -rose, -indigo) now carry commit-moment chromaticity at the jewel level; the icon roster expands from three recipes to five, with R4 (filled jewel disc) and R5 (single-hue bicolor stack) the two new sanctioned places light tint can appear — and only paired with a deep stop of the same hue. Member CTAs deepen one stop (primary.600 → primary.700), which lifts the AA ratio to AAA almost everywhere without any visible darkening cost. The AI surface drops the indigo wash in favor of a sand surface with indigo border + tag — same label, less wash competition.

How it positions us: alongside Hinge and Sword in Clinical-Modern Distinctive (locked tribe), but with a more confident chromatic register than v4's earth-leaning restraint. v4 reads "calm, editorial, restrained." v5 reads "credible, deliberate, present." The credibility floor lifts — categoricals at 700 stops feel like a clinical brand making decisions, not a wellness brand reaching for warmth. If v4 was the reset, v5 is the system asking whether restraint can carry more weight when the chromatic punctuation lives deeper.
