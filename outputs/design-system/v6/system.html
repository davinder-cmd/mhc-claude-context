The pull-back

v5 tested a Jewel-accent register against a Neutral base — categoricals at 600/700 as saturated stops on sand surfaces. Designer feedback: jewel reads heavy at scale, and lavender + indigo in particular sit wrong — purples want air, not punctuation. v6 holds the v5 architectural lesson (sand + neutral split protects from muddle) but scales the chromatic register down: categoricals retire to 100–200 stops as light accents, gradients retire entirely from product, purples demote to glyph and dot only, and the green family (moss + success) gets the most screen time when chroma surfaces. The result re-aligns with v4's editorial restraint, with the muddle protection v5 invented carrying forward.

Stakeholder narrative

v6 is a refinement of v5. v5 tested whether color and tone could survive a Jewel-accent register — categoricals at 600/700 stops as saturated punctuation on sand surfaces. The architectural insight held (the muddle protection still works), but at applied scale the jewel weight read heavy, and the purple-family hues — lavender (Health Data) and indigo (AI marker) — sat wrong: purples want air, not punctuation.

What carried forward unchanged: the R2 sky-teal bridge primary at #2A6489 (locked under D2), the neutral-on-sand split (HC-3, the muddle protection), the Clinical-Modern Distinctive tribe (D1), the cross-cutting AI principle (indigo means AI, regardless of host module), the dot+caps editorial badges, the variant-grid illustration economy, and the open-source Material Symbols Outlined library.

What v6 changes: the color register pulls back from Neutral-base + Jewel-accents to plain Neutral. Categoricals retire to 100–200 stops in product surfaces; gradients retire entirely from product (member or buyer); purples (lavender + indigo) demote to glyph, dot, sparkline, and border — never as a card or wash. The icon roster holds at five recipes, but R4's filled disc shifts container from 600/700 to 500. The member CTA reverts primary.700 → primary.600 (v4 default). The buyer hero retires its deep-primary gradient in favor of a flat primary.800 surface. The AI surface drops to sand + indigo border + indigo tag, re-aligning with v4's Rule 5. Within the categoricals, the green family (moss + success) gets the largest share of chromatic real estate — DCP earns a moss-50/100 container surface; rose and fuchsia recede in applied moments.

How v6 positions: alongside Hinge and Sword in Clinical-Modern Distinctive (locked tribe), but pulling toward the editorial-restraint pole rather than the chromatic-confidence pole v5 explored. v4 read "calm, editorial, restrained." v5 read "credible, deliberate, present, chromatic." v6 reads "credible, calm, present" — chromatic confidence tempered by editorial discipline. If v4 was the reset and v5 was the chromatic-stretch test, v6 is the consolidation: keep the muddle-protection architecture, drop the chromatic surface area to a level that lets photography and typography carry the warmth.
