My lean

Where I'd land

Option A is the most cohesive — collapsing the card and the "Start" button into one tappable object is the single biggest declutter, and it keeps the "one clear next" conviction from D1 while staying confident and image-forward. Option B is the safer, calmer pick if you want an explicit labeled button and less imagery — it's closest to your readability-first instinct. Option C is worth it only if research says people want a choice at this moment; it trades a little focus for a little agency. I'd ship A, keep B as the fallback, and hold C unless testing asks for choice.

All three keep the approved top (celebration → $100 → cap meter) and honor the locked decisions: D1 (one clear next + browse open), D2 (celebrate the finish), D3 (EBB leads), D4 (next from interests). Only the bottom third changes. Screen v2 — bottom options for reaction.
