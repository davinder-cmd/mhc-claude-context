Same screen, other states

Variants to design next

This is the everyday completion. The same layout flexes to a few important edge states:

First-ever completion

An extra beat — a bigger moment. "Your first care path. Here's your first $100." First-time moments punch above their weight.

Cap reached (5 of 5)

The meter is full. Stop dangling payout; switch the tappable card to health framing — "You've earned the max this year; this one's still worth doing."

No strong next match

When interests don't point to an obvious next path, the card softens to "Explore care paths" rather than forcing a weak recommendation.
