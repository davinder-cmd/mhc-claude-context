Engagement-Based Billing · Get-In flow

How each user opts in to EBB

Three cohorts, three layers. The first touch scales in intensity by who they are; a persistent home entry catches anyone who skips; and everyone lands at the same contextual opt-in, where the real consent is captured. Importance drives prominence — interruption is used sparingly.

Where all three meet

Consent is always captured at the DCP — the point of value

The announcement and the banner build awareness; the binding consent (HIPAA/ADA) and the 50% eligibility are captured contextually, when the member goes to continue or start a program. That keeps the intro light and the real decision where the reward is.

The one lever that changes this flow

Open decision #1 — hard-gate vs. earn-gate. If earn-gate: the contextual opt-in is a dismissible modal — members can keep doing the program and opt in later to earn. If hard-gate: it's a blocking wall — no further access without consent. This single call sets whether the mid-DCP modal and the contextual gate are dismissible or mandatory. Needs commercial + legal, not design.

Principle: importance → prominence, not automatic interruption. Interrupt only twice — the one-time mid-DCP intro and the consent decision. Everyone else gets a prominent, calm home card + persistent banner. Mirrors the consent workstream in `projects/feature-ebb/_brief.md`. Get-In flow v1 — for reaction.
