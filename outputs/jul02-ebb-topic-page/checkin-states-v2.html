An outcome check-in, not a progress meter

Updated after the charter discussion. This block answers "is it working?" — the member's real goal — separate from "am I getting through it?" It's the member-facing face of measurement-based care: it speaks up at check-in points, celebrates real improvement, and — most importantly — turns "not improving" into help arriving, never a bad grade.

What changed from v1

1 · Event-driven — it's tied to the program's assessment checkpoints, not shown loudly on every view. Between checkpoints it rests.

2 · The check-in prompt is now a state — capturing the measure (2 min) is part of the block, so the signal is real.

3 · "Needs support" is a doorway — non-response surfaces a coach/Anna (and safety routing), never a score. This is the clinical value: catching it early.

The rhythm — event-driven
Baseline · Session 1 → Rests between → Check-in · Session 5 → Result: improving / support → Rests → Check-in · Session 8
The block only makes a claim when there's a fresh measure to back it. That keeps it honest and high-signal.

The charter (locked)

Why this block exists — the rules it follows

Purpose

Reflects outcome ("is it working?"), separate from progress ("am I getting through it?"). The face of measurement-based care.

Only if we act on it

Kept only because it drives a response (celebrate, or surface help). No decorative positivity — if we won't act, we cut it.

Good / bad is clinical

Thresholds are clinician-owned (reliable change), not arbitrary. Design renders; clinical sets.

Show "bad" as a doorway

Non-response → a coach/Anna (+ safety routing), never a verdict or number. Catching it early is the point.
