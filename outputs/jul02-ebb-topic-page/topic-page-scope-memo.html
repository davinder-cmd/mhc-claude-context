Keep the DCP Topic Page Focused on Completion — Where Data & Resources Live in EBB

To: Product & Innovations (topic-page scope) — _edit to the specific owner of the data/resources request_ From: Davinder Rehal Date: 2026-07-02

Context

There's a push to add relevant health data and resource links to the DCP topic page. As we build the Engagement-Based Billing (EBB) experience, we need to settle the topic page's scope now — before it drifts back into a content hub.

Recommendation

Keep the topic page focused on one job — getting the member into their next session — and place data and resources where they help without competing with that action. This is a placement decision, not a rejection: the content still lives in the product, just not featured on the topic page.

Rationale

  • This page is the revenue engine. Under EBB we bill on session completion and pay the incentive on topic completion. The topic page exists to advance that. Every element that isn't the loop competes for attention with the one action that gets everyone paid.
  • Attention is zero-sum, and more content ≠ better outcomes. Adding data + links increases decision time and dilutes the primary CTA (Hick's Law, Von Restorff, Cognitive Load). The engagement-loop research also found an "engagement–efficacy gap" — busier screens drive neither completion nor health outcomes, and can undermine them.
  • A content-hub topic page reverts the strategy. The whole EBB bet — make the next step effortless — is the deliberate move away from the old browse-everything model. This isn't a small addition; it's a reversal of the direction we've already locked.

Where it lives instead

Content typeWhere it goesOn the topic page?
Health data / metricsThe trackerAt most one topic-relevant insight (e.g., "mood trending up") — reinforces the health why
"Learn more" / resourcesInside the lesson and via Anna's "I don't get this" Q&A — contextual, exactly when neededA clearly secondary entry below the loop, if at all — never competing with Continue

The test for anything proposed for this page: "Does it help them start the next session?" If yes, it belongs. If it's nice-to-have-while-they're-here, it routes to the tracker, the lesson, or Anna.

Tradeoffs Considered

OptionWhy Not
Topic page as a data + resource hubDilutes the primary action, adds decision cost, and reverts the browse-everything model EBB moved away from — hurts the completion metric the page exists to drive.
Omit data & resources entirelyMembers genuinely need both — so we relocate rather than remove. Data → tracker; resources → lesson/Anna.

Ask

Sign off that the DCP topic page stays loop-focused, and that data/resource requests route to the tracker, the lesson, or Anna rather than onto this page. Bring specific must-have items and we'll place them against the test above.
