The path · sessions

Show the shape, not all 10

Completed carry a check, current is marked, next is locked — the journey read at a glance. Windowed to the immediately-prior + current + next, then "See all 10."

  • First session: a clear "Start" + two locked previews (no checks yet).
  • Final session: earlier sessions collapse to "Sessions 1–8 complete," and a 🏁 finish marker caps the path — the payout is the end of the road.

How you're doing · outcome check-in

Is it working? — measurement-based care

Separate from progress: this reflects outcome (are they getting better), the member's real goal — what makes this a care path, not a tracker. Plain language ("feeling better"), no clinical jargon or score. The assessment powers it behind the scenes.

  • Event-driven — speaks up at check-in points, rests between (shown here: an improving result).
  • State-aware: Resting · Check-in due (2-min capture) · Improving · Needs support.
  • "Bad" = a doorway, never a verdict — non-response surfaces Anna/a coach; a safety threshold routes to crisis. Kept only because we act on it.
