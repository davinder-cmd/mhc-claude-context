One screen, one scroll — the $300 and the $100 invite together.

Option A — Savor first

Pro: the $300 win gets a clean, undiluted moment (peak-end); the pivot feels like a fresh, positive offer. Con: an extra screen — some drop off before seeing the EBB invite.

Option B — Combined

Pro: nobody misses the EBB invite — it's on the screen everyone sees; fewer taps. Con: the $100 sits right under $300 and may feel small by comparison; busier moment.

What to test

Same metric both ways: % who go from assessment-complete → start an EBB care path. A tells us if a clean $300 moment converts better; B tells us if guaranteed exposure wins. Lean A for the first read (protect the $300 peak), with B as the challenger.
