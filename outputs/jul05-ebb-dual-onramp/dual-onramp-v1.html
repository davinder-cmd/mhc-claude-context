Why two on-ramps

What this buys us

Widest funnel

No-$300 clients, users who skip the assessment, and existing members all still have a way into EBB. Matches Darcy's "widest possible funnel" + risk-default-off.

De-risks 9/1

EBB doesn't wait on the assessment being funded or ready. Lane B can ship on its own; Lane A is the upgrade when the assessment is in play.

The tradeoff

Without the $300 hook, the $100 carries motivation alone and the recommendation is weaker (interests, not risk). Lane B leans harder on the surfacing kit + easy browse.

Dual on-ramp v1. Lane A (assessment-driven, with $300, risk-grounded) and Lane B (interest/browse, no assessment, $100-motivated) converge on one opt-in → shared loop. Decision: assessment = accelerant, not prerequisite (see `_decisions.md`, 2026-07-05).
