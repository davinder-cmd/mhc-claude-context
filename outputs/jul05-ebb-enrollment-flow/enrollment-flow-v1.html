Step 1 · Home

The invitation

Rides the existing home components (hero banner / "Just For You") — home is out of 9/1 scope. Framing: "see if you qualify to earn $100." Prominent, not a takeover.

Existing components (9/1)
Step 2 · Health check

The measure

12 questions across 5 pillars — the natural handoff point. Short and low-friction so people finish it.

Assessment handoff
Step 3 · Recommendation

"You may qualify"

Assessment → a matched DCP + the earn opportunity. The at-risk / interest signal drives the recommendation. Note: can't pre-populate the DCP from assessment yet (data siloed).

Enrollment daisy-chain
Step 4 · Opt in

Consent, value-forward

The reward terms + consent (HIPAA/ADA) at the point of value. "$100 Amazon gift card · up to $500/yr." Whether this is dismissible or a wall = the hard-gate vs. earn-gate decision.

Consent gate
Step 5 · Unlocked

Into the loop

Reward unlocked → dropped onto the topic page at Session 1. The enrollment funnel hands off to the completion loop we've already built.

The reward, two ways

Capped vs. no cap — both configs

The cap is a client-configurable setting. The system default is no cap; the recommended best-practice config is $500/year. Here's how the EBB reward reads in each.

Recommended Capped · $500/year
Care Path Rewards · $100 Amazon gift card each
$100of $500 this year
1 of 5 care paths — earn another $100 gift card with your next.

Bounded goal-gradient: the 5-pip meter shows how much is left ($400 / 4 paths). Clear ceiling; graceful "max reached" state at 5 of 5.

System default No cap
Care Path Rewards · $100 Amazon gift card each
$100earned this year
$100 per completed care path
Complete another program to earn another $100 gift card.

Open-ended: no ceiling, no pips — just cumulative earned + the per-path value. The pull is "another $100," not "X of $500." Never a "max reached" state.
