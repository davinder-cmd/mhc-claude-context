Reading the map

Three things it makes clear

The money ladder

$300 once for the assessment (knowing) → $100 per care path (doing), up to $500/yr. A member could earn up to $800 in year one. The $300 is the hook; the $100s are the engine.

Get in once, loop forever

Phase 1 happens a single time. Phase 2 is the durable engine — every trip through it is a completion (revenue) and a re-hook into the next path.

Two open gates

Opt-in (6): hard-gate vs. earn-gate — still the pivotal decision. At-risk model (5): risk stratification default (config). Both are commercial/legal calls, awaiting Darcy's requirements.
