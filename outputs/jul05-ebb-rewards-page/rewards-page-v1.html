Page charter

EBB leads; money is allowed to be loud here

Opposite of the topic page. There, we kept the $100 to one calm mention (crowding-out risk on a health screen). Here it's the rewards page — leading with the dollar figure is honest and expected. The other reward types stay, but clearly subordinate.

Key state to design next

Cap reached (5 of 5 · $500)

When the meter fills, we stop dangling payout — the hero flips to "You've earned the max this year ($500)" and the CTA shifts from "earn $100 more" to health framing ("keep going — here's what's worth doing next"). Same rule as the completion screen: never dangle a reward that can't be paid.
