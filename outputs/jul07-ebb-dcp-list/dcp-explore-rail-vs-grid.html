Explore more paths — carousel vs grid (mobile)

The "Explore more paths" section is a small curated set (the full 14 live behind "See all"). Two ways to show ~4 on mobile. Carousels aren't inherently wrong at MHC — the failure mode is peek-cards that hide content with no clear affordance. For a curated few, a wrapping grid avoids that entirely.
