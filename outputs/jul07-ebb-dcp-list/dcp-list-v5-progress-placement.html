One progress banner up top (faint track + milestone dots, Airalo-style) carries the year's story — paths + $ toward the cap. The cards below go quiet: just "Session 6 of 10" text. Calmest, and the reward/progress reads once, clearly. Cost: per-path progress is only text.
Honest to our data: both show only sessions completed (what we actually track) — no fabricated "% adherence" or finer signal. A makes each program feel substantial (good when we want presence per path); B is calmer and lets the reward-across-all read once (good for resume-first + pace-not-speed). They're combinable: B's top banner + A's ring on just the single emphasized "Continue" card.
