When nothing's started — no empty "Continue" staring back. A light reward hook, strong recommendations up top (why-tagged: health check / popular), then browse opens up by area (multiple paths, not one rail), and more recs at the bottom. Browse is a first-class citizen here, not a demoted link.
Threading the needle: one page, two states driven by "is anything in progress?" The reward banner also adapts — a progress meter when they've earned, an invitation ("earn up to $500/yr") when they haven't. The empty state doubles as the natural Lane B / starting-point surface without becoming a home page: it stays care-paths-only. As paths get started, discovery recedes and Continue takes over — and the browse they used lives on behind "Browse by area / See all," so it's never truly hidden.
