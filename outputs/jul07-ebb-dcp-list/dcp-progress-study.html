Your Airalo reference — a faint track with milestone dots on it, fill in teal to the current point. Dots mark meaningful moments (halfway, complete), not every session — cleaner than 10 dots, scales to any length, and the "halfway" node can double as the ~50% reward-eligible marker. To simplify: Option 6 (faint track + milestone dots) and Option 2 (ring) are the strongest. Option 6 stays calm via the faint track, scales to any length, and its dots carry meaning (halfway = the ~50% reward-eligible point, complete) instead of counting every session — a nice tie to the 50% rule. Option 2 (ring) is the most compact. Option 3 (dots + current line) suits shorter programs; Option 5 is the segmented compromise. Drop the plain filling bar (Option 1) — it reads as a countdown.
