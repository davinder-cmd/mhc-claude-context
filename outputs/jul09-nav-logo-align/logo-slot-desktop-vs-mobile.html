Logo slot — desktop is left-aligned, mobile is centered

Same bounding-box idea, two different anchors. The earlier diagram showed the square mark centered inside the slot, which implied it floats in the middle of the bar. It doesn't — on desktop the logo hugs the left gutter.

Correction: desktop is a max-width, hug-to-content model — the logo container snaps to the logo's actual width (capped at 240px) and the nav links sit right after it, snug (with padding). A logo-mark-only client is ~48px wide, flush-left, links immediately following — not a fixed 240px box, and never centered. Mobile is the centered one. The two are separated below.

Desktop top bar anchor: left

Logo sits at the left gutter. It scales to fit the box — filling to whichever limit it hits first — but always anchored left. Short marks do not center.

Mobile top bar anchor: center

On mobile the logo is centered, and the frame follows each platform's standard bar. Both use a 160-unit max-width box with the mark centered inside — the mark fills to max-height, wordmarks scale down to the 160 cap.
