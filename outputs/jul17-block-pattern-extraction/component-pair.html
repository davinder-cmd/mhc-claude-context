Pair card

A titled recommendation card — thumbnail + title/meta row + text-link footer, with a whole-card stretched link. Two sit side by side ("Based on your interests" / "From your employer"): one column on phones, two from tablet. At ≥1200 the thumb grows from an 80² marker to a 160×120 4:3 photo.

Markup

One pair card (interest variant). The .pair grid holds up to two, each gated by its own flag so a single card degrades gracefully. Footer "See more / Edit" are navigations (<a>), not buttons.

Notes & flags

• Adaptive thumb: 80×80 below 1200px, 160×120 4:3 at ≥1200 — the photo only earns real estate at the top-nav target width.
• Footer alignment: negative margins pull the first/last text-link so their visible text sits flush with the card edge (Material card-action pattern).
• Interactive footer links sit above the stretched link (z-index:2) so both remain clickable.
