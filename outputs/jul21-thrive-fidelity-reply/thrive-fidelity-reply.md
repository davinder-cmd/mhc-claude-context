Useful comparison, and I agree in-page refresh is worth prioritizing — it's a real gap and a good near-term win.

One thing I want to flag so it doesn't get lost: Amy's feedback is broader than any single feature. In-page refresh covers part of the "dynamic" piece, but I suspect much of what she calls out points to fidelity: the craft and consistency of how the experience is executed. Uniform spacing, padding, and standardized components — with consistent performance underneath — are what make an app feel like one coherent, polished system instead of a set of one-off pages. That consumer-grade coherence is the baseline people now expect — and when it's missing, that's exactly what reads as "not modern, engaging, or resonant."

We had fidelity as a priority earlier this year and it's drifted. Worth not overlooking.
