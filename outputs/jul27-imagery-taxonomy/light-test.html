Lighting & contrast — measured, then shown

Every file measured for exposure, contrast and colour, then grouped by what it breaks. The three toggles are the point: highlight clipping paints pure-white pixels red the way a camera's zebra display does, and white text + scrim show whether a photograph can carry overlaid type — the practical consequence of a pale frame.
