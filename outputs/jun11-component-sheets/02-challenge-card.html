Challenge card · variants

The single-banner challenge module that sits below the data section. Covers active, recently-completed, and edge-case states. Always one banner — when multiple challenges are active, only the most recently engaged-with is featured here and the rest live behind the drill-in.

View in Figma

Variants

All variants share the shell: 56-px disc icon on the left, body in the middle (eyebrow + title + meta), drill-in or action on the right.

  • Active — eyebrow TEAM CHALLENGE, title Spring Steps Challenge, meta "Day 22 of 30 · Currently 4th". Trophy icon. Whole card is the tap-target.
  • Completed — eyebrow Completed · May 13, title same, meta "3rd place — finished strong" (or similar finish line). CTA on the right reads See leaderboard instead of the drill-in chevron.
  • Final day — eyebrow gains "Final day" sub-text under the section title; banner copy emphasizes urgency.
  • No challenge active — empty state. Soft prompt to discover/join a challenge. Different shell — flat copy + CTA to browse.
  • Multiple challenges — same single-banner shell, but the section head adds a small "2 active" indicator next to "Your challenges" to hint that more exist behind the drill-in.

Notes

  • Icon disc is 56 × 56 px on both desktop and mobile (bumped to match the rewards banner disc). Earlier the disc was hidden on mobile — that hide rule has been removed for the challenge banner.
  • Trophy glyph is Font Awesome &#xf091;. For "no challenge" empty state, swap for a different glyph (or remove the icon altogether).
  • The stretched .challenge__link makes the whole card tappable — keep the visible body text as the accessible name; the link's visually-hidden label is for screen readers.
