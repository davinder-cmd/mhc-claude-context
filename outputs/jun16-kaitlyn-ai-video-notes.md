# Meeting Notes — AI Video & Lesson Content

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**With:** Kaitlyn

---

## Easy-to-express findings

- **We have a content consistency problem.** The new AI video scripts are meaningfully different from the existing written lessons, so showing video, transcript, and original content together creates multiple versions of the same lesson.

- **The current AI-video approach risks feeling less credible.** The scripts are more conversational and "podcasty" than the current content, and there was concern that the AI avatar format could undermine trust.

- **The product requirement is too broad.** Applying video to every lesson with audio does not fit short, repetitive, or utility-style content like assessments or tracking tasks.

- **Talking-head video alone is not enough for instructional content.** Lessons with visual aids or physical exercises need demonstration and supporting visuals, not just an avatar speaking.

- **The right long-term model is likely an integrated experience, not separate assets.** The discussion pointed toward a flow where video, chat, and user input work together in one experience rather than as disconnected pages.

- **For now, the practical move is to simplify for the demo.** The likely near-term approach is to unify the content for demo purposes and avoid having to explain conflicting versions.

- **Immediate next step: clarify scope with Darcy.** The main decision is whether this is just a demo exploration or something being built more fully in v1, and requirements should be revisited before going too far.
