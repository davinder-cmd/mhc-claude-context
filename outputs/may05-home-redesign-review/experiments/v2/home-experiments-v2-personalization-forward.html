MHC Home — Experiment v2 · Personalization Forward

v2 · 2026-05-06 · Block fidelity · Desktop 1200 + Mobile 375 · One experiment

A second experiment applying a new prominence ranking: the two source-labeled lanes ("Things I care about" + "From your employer") are promoted to weight 4, immediately below the hero. Programs is demoted to weight 3 and narrowed editorially to DCPs only (no journeys, no habits, no challenges-in-progress). Tracker and Insights are paired into a single mid-page module. Tests: does putting personalization second on the page — visibly demonstrating the data we have — outperform putting member momentum (programs) second?

This v2 is a counter-bet to v1's "Programs anchors momentum." v1 says members come back to continue their work — so the second-most-prominent surface is what they're already doing. v2 says personalization is the differentiator — so the second-most-prominent surface visibly responds to who they told us they are and what their employer wants them to see. Pick by which signal you want the page to lead with after the hero.

Reflow decisions

  • Lanes (4 + 5): 50/50 paired desktop → stacked mobile, Things first then Employer (collapse, no reorder)
  • Tracker + Insights: single combined module both breakpoints; tiles 4-up desktop → 2×2 mobile
  • Programs (DCP only): 3-up DCP cards desktop → 2 stacked + see-all mobile
  • No "+ New challenge available" tile inside Programs. v1 had this; v2 removes it because Challenges is a separate section and Programs is now editorially DCP-only.
  • Habits and journeys do not appear on home in v2. Live in their own tabs/pages. Surface on home would dilute the DCP clarity.

When this wins · risks

Wins when: personalization is your competitive bet (visibly demonstrate "we listened to onboarding") · employer engagement metrics matter to client renewals · sales wants a permanent labeled employer surface that doesn't depend on hero allocation · the DCP catalog is rich and clinically credible (so the narrowed Programs section feels deep, not thin).

Risks: non-enrolled members (no DCPs) get a thin Programs section — needs strong empty state ("Find a care path matched to your goals") · journey + habit users may feel their work is hidden — flag for member feedback · two w4 sections side-by-side risk visual competition; UI must handle the lane separation cleanly · pushes tracker further down the page; members who came to log/check tracker data have to scroll past lanes.

Editorial implications · DCP-only programs

  • What appears in Programs: active DCPs only (Sleep, Stress, Diabetes Prevention, Diabetes Management, Anxiety, Depression, Back Pain — per the DCP catalog in feature-dcp/)
  • What doesn't: journeys, habits, assessments-in-progress, active challenges
  • Where they live instead: journeys → dedicated tab/profile · habits → Healthy Habits feature · challenges → section 8 (Challenges) · assessments → standalone HRA flow
  • Empty state for non-enrolled: shift section title to "Care paths for you" + show 2–3 algorithmic DCP recommendations + CTA to take HRA. Don't render an empty programs band.
  • Sales / CS framing: this narrowing makes the section the clinical-depth story (DCPs are MHC's clinical-program differentiator vs. Personify's lighter "Journeys"). Lean into it.

v1 → v2 recommendation

Hold both as live candidates. Apply UI to v1 Exp 1 (Calm Anchor) as control and v2 Personalization Forward as the personalization-bet. They differ on a clear axis (programs vs. lanes as second-most-prominent surface) — when UI is applied, the choice will make itself obvious to stakeholders looking at side-by-side comps.

Don't try to merge the two. The whole point of v2 is the bet on personalization-as-second-tier. Hedge that and you get the v1 baseline back.
