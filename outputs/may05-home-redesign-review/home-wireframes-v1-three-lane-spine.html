Mobile annotations
  • Hero is single-committed, not carousel. Phase 1 attainable. Image cropped to top half of the 50/50 split asset — keeps subject in frame without overlay text. Source label ("Active challenge") makes provenance legible.
  • Rewards chip in top-right is dollar-framed, not point-framed. "$75 · $25 to next" is the differentiator vs. Personify's raw point counts. Tap → rewards detail.
  • No focus pill at page-level. The pill appears only inline, on the Interests lane label, where it actually scopes content (per direction update 03).
  • Today strip is 3 tiles (Steps / Sleep / Heart) — 4th tile (Active Minutes) only on wider breakpoints. 3 fits the mobile width without truncation.
  • DCP card includes the clinical metric inline ("pain down 22%"). Personify can't do this — their programs are content. This is the proof-of-value module.
  • Two lanes are stacked, employer first (signals the page isn't all about the user — there's content from the company too), then interests. Each lane carries its own source label and uses the green/blue accent strip on the left edge.
  • Healthy Habits shows point values inline per Personify's "every CTA carries a point value" pattern (which we lost in Jill's converged direction).
  • Benefits is in the employer lane, with a concrete dollar value ("$0 mental health visits"). Beyond Personify — they have nothing equivalent.

Vector Personify This wireframe Why it wins
Reward framing Raw point counts ("5,200 / 10,000") Dollar-equivalent header chip + slim row ("$75 earned · $25 to next gift card") Gift cards are 4–10× more valuable than their points; show the dollars, not the abstraction
Program proof Generic content "Journeys" · no clinical metric DCP Progress card with inline clinical change ("pain down 22%") We have measurable interventions; surface the measurement
Content provenance Opaque feed ("For You") Two explicit lanes · "From your employer" / "Based on your interests" · per-item source tags Trust signal; user can read why something is showing
AI PercyIQ — explains without acting (G2 critiqued) One takeaway + one specific action ("Add wind-down to my evening") Closing the loop is the differentiator, not the AI itself
Benefits visibility Nothing equivalent Employer lane surfaces benefits with concrete dollar value ("$0 mental health visits") Most users don't know what's covered — solving information asymmetry is real value
Per-action point/dollar values Inline on every action ("Set a wellbeing goal — 600 Points") Inline on every action with dollar progress ("earns $25 at challenge end") Adopt their pattern, escalate the unit. No CTA without a value attached.

Net engineering ask for Track A (Phase 1 ship): 1 new component — the persistent rewards header chip. Everything else assembles from existing Page Layout Elements. Track B (Phase 2) adds the dots-carousel hero and the top-nav chrome rebuild.

Open decisions before this becomes a spec

  • Rewards header chip: Confirm engineering will build a persistent cross-page chip. If they push back, fall back to placing the rewards slim row as the first module on every primary tab — heavier lift but visually solves the same problem.
  • Hero in Phase 1: Single committed only, no dots. If the priority engine has multiple high-priority items in a session, second-priority items demote to the lane area. Acceptable trade for Phase 1 simplicity.
  • Today strip metric set: Defaults shown are Steps + Sleep + Heart Rate (mobile) and + Active Minutes (desktop). Confirm Heart Rate availability across the wearable mix; substitute Active Minutes if not.
  • Lane content tagging: The two-lane model assumes content is tagged by source ("From your employer" vs. "Based on your interests"). Some current content may not be tagged — confirm Innovations / Client Services workflow can do this at content-publish time.
  • Mobile lane order: Showing employer first to break the "page is all about me" reading. Test against showing interests first — A/B if budget allows.
  • Benefits in employer lane: Worth confirming with Darcy that surfacing benefit detail on home isn't already a Benefits-tab decision being made elsewhere. Don't duplicate work.

What's deliberately not here

  • No focus pill at page level. Pill appears only inline on the Interests lane label. Page-level focus anchoring caused the "page lies about its scope" defect (per direction update 03).
  • No Chat Advisor. Out of John's ask, scope creep, deferred to Q3 conversation (per critique 02).
  • No "no-focus state" branch. Picker is mandatory (1–3 selections per Granola); no skip path means no need for fallback states.
  • No Welcome banner. Greeting headline does the same job (per critique 02).
  • No Trophy Case / achievements gallery. Personify pattern, deferred to small follow-up (per Personify analysis 04).
  • No biometrics on Today strip. Clinical screening data (BP, A1C) is detail-screen only; Today is daily ambient tracker data (per assumptions 01).
  • No daily educational quiz cards. Personify pattern, would dilute our DCP depth differentiator (per Personify analysis 04).

How John's Top 5 are addressed

  • #1 Logo center top: ✓ centered on every breakpoint. Phase 1 above the collapsed-left-rail content area; Phase 2 left-of-nav-items in the top bar.
  • #2 Side nav → top/bottom: ✓ Phase 1 collapses to icon rail; Phase 2 removes entirely in favor of top nav. Mobile uses bottom-tab.
  • #3 Carousel replacement: ✓ Phase 1: single committed hero (no carousel). Phase 2: dots-carousel done right (dots below, not peek-cards). Critically, the failure mode of the old carousel (peek + no JS) is the thing being fixed, not multiplicity itself.
  • #4 Spinner + 1s threshold: Component-level; not in this wireframe pass. Standalone ticket.
  • #5 Tracker perf audit: Data-fetch level; not in this wireframe pass. Today strip renders skeleton tiles in <100ms while values stream — design provides the rendering contract.
