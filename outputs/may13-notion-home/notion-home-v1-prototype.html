FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between Notion AI and Agents?
Notion AI is in-line writing and Q&A — it lives next to your cursor. Agents are persistent workers you delegate to: they run on a schedule, take multi-step actions, and report back. Same models, different surface area.
Can I try Notion before paying?
Yes. The Free plan is generous enough for individuals and small teams to run real work indefinitely. Paid plans add seats, integrations, and admin controls.
How does Notion handle our data?
SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR-aligned. Customer data is never used to train models without explicit opt-in. Enterprise customers get region-pinned data residency.
What if we already use Slack, Google Workspace, or GitHub?
Notion plugs in alongside them — sync databases from GitHub or Slack, embed Google Docs, and let agents read across the stack. You don't have to migrate to get value.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Annual plans are prorated on cancellation; monthly plans end at the close of the current billing period. Your data stays exportable, free or paid.
