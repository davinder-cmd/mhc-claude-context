Healthy Habits

Why this works for page headers: the title and any controls below land at a predictable Y position regardless of viewport. Engineering can rely on the header being exactly 240px tall. The trade-off: aggressive cropping on mobile, so pick photos with the focal point near center.

When NOT to use this pattern

Avoid fixed-height for: editorial cards, content tiles, anything in a grid. The shape-changing crop behavior makes images feel inconsistent across breakpoints — what looked balanced on desktop looks awkwardly tight on mobile. Fixed-height is only safe when the container is full-width AND the photo's focal point is centered/predictable. For most other cases, use Pattern 1 (aspect-ratio locked) or Pattern 3 (hybrid).
