Treating Insomnia

8 sessions Not started

Insomnia is more common than you might think. Over 40 million Americans have chronic insomnia, and 30% of the population report some insomnia. This program guides you through evidence-based techniques from cognitive behavioral therapy.

What you'll learn

Across 8 sessions, roughly 15 minutes each

Practice evidence-based techniques from cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I). You'll build a personalized wind-down routine, learn to retrain your sleep associations, and use a sleep diary to track changes over the program.
