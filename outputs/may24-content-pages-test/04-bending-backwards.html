Bending backwards

Bending backward exercises can correct the soft tissues in your lower back that have moved out of position or that have distorted in shape.

Assess your range of motion

First assess your back's range of motion. Look at yourself in the mirror and stand with the correct posture. Slide your hands slowly down the front of your legs and see how far back you can bend comfortably.

Next, place your hands in the arch in your lower back and slowly bend backwards, also gauging how far you can bend.

Summary

  1. 1

    Backward bending exercises can correct changes in soft tissues in your back that can cause pain and discomfort.

  2. 2

    First assess your back's range of motion to see if you feel restricted and to be able to gauge improvements.
