## Summary of findings

Google Health is what Fitbit became when it grew up inside Google:

- **Material You** is the operating language — soft lavender, mint, peach, butter; bright Google blue/green/yellow/red reserved for primary moments only
- **Four tabs** anchor the IA: Today · Fitness · Sleep · Health. Today and Health are user-customizable
- **Gemini Health Coach** is the strategy, not a feature — conic-gradient avatar, conversational chat, proactive insights, suggestion chips
- **Soft renames:** Stress score → Resilience (Optimal / Balanced / Low). Menstrual health → Cycle health. Numeric scores replaced with verdict words
- **Photography-led** marketing — diverse subjects in aspirational athleisure: lavender activewear, golden-hour stretches, living-room yoga
- **Activity rings return** — but in Google's red/green/blue Material You palette, not Apple's
- **Gradient-word treatment** — single key words ("Resilience," "Cycle health," "Coach") get the blue→lavender→green Gemini gradient

## One steal · one to study · one strategic call

1. **Steal — soft language for clinical metrics.** Renaming Stress → Resilience, with Optimal / Balanced / Low replacing a number, is the smartest copy decision of the year. Our DCP outcome metrics should follow.
2. **Study — the single-word gradient.** A disciplined chromatic treatment for any word the AI surfaces "owns." We could mark our AI surfaces (Insights, DCP Expert, Motivator Coach) consistently this way.
3. **Strategic call — don't compete with the Coach on chat.** Pixel + Google Health Coach will be the default conversational AI for a chunk of our member base. Our edge is the program, the clinical evidence base, and cross-condition trust — not out-chatting Gemini.
