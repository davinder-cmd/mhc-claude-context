## Summary of findings

Sword Health's design DNA is premium-accessible healthtech earned through restraint:

- **Color:** cream + navy stage, one signature orange (~12% of the canvas) doing all the energy work, a discreet teal reserved for AI moments
- **Type:** geometric sans, tight letterspacing, 48–96px display, single-word color jump as the only flourish
- **Imagery:** documentary-style — workers in warehouses, kitchens, train cabs — natural light, real environments, no stock-wellness gloss
- **Voice:** mission first ("a world-class clinician in every home"), evidence second, product third
- **AI:** deliberately rejects the neural-network cliché — AI shows up as conversational bubbles ("Phoenix"), not dashboards

## Three moves to steal

1. Single accent color, used with discipline
2. Real-people documentary photography in workplace settings
3. Outcome-as-hero — one massive stat, one clinical claim

## One move to reject

Sword's salvific register ("free the world from pain") fits acute MSK. MHC's ICP — the overburdened employee managing 2–3 conditions privately — needs warmth without messianic framing.
