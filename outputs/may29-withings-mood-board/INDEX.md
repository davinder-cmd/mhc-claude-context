## Summary of findings

Withings Health Mate is the quietest, most data-led health app in the category — the antithesis of motivational fitness apps:

- **Surface:** cool lavender-tinted gray (#F3F4F8), white cards, near-black ink — no warmth, no cream
- **Accent:** one signature periwinkle indigo (~#5E5CE6) — used for sparklines, CTAs, the Withings X mark
- **Pastels:** four category chips (Activity butter, Body lilac, Heart pink, Cycle peach) handle the entire visual energy budget
- **Type:** numbers set 60–96px and bold; section headers smaller than the metric values
- **Voice in-app:** reduces to one-word verdicts — "Stable." "Normal." "High HR."
- **Imagery:** almost no photography. Editorial flat illustration only, retro/French-magazine register
- **Data viz:** small indigo sparklines with dot nodes, muted vertical bars, distinctive cycle-tracking coral disc clusters — no rings, no gauges, no big stat cards on the free tier
- **Signature atomic unit:** icon → label → huge number → one-word status → sparkline, repeated 8–12× per dashboard

## Two moves to steal

1. **Number-as-hero** — 96px metric value, one-word status under it. The most economical pattern in the category. Our wearable cards under-display the numbers.
2. **Pastel category chips** — four small pastel cards handle top-level IA without competing color systems. Could work for program areas.

## One to study

**Illustration over photography.** Withings can get away with editorial illustration because their hardware does the photography work on the marketing site. We can't — our members need to see themselves represented.

## One to reject

**The one-word app voice.** "Stable. Normal." works for the quantified-self lifestyle buyer. Our overburdened-employee ICP needs one more sentence of context than Withings gives. Restraint yes, silence no.
