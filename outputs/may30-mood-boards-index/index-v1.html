Platform Study · Just shipped

Google Health — a friendlier OS for wellness.

Launched eleven days ago, replacing the Fitbit app. Material You + a Gemini-powered Health Coach. The smartest copy decision of the year: "Stress score" became "Resilience" (Optimal / Balanced / Low). The strategic question for MHC isn't whether to compete with the Coach — it's where not to.

Mission Study · MSK competitor

Sword Health — clinical authority, human warmth.

Premium healthtech earned through restraint, not ornament. Cream + navy stage, one signature orange, documentary photography of real workers in real workplaces. The brand reads as healthcare mission with a tech backbone — not the other way around.

Hardware Study · Quantified-self

Withings Health Mate — numbers as protagonist.

The quietest health app in the category. Cool lavender surface, one periwinkle indigo, almost no photography — editorial flat illustration only. The number is the story, paired with a tiny sparkline and a one-word verdict. Restraint as luxury signal.

At a glance

Three brands. Three different jobs.

The most useful frame after reading all three: each of these brands solved a different positioning problem with visual restraint, and the answers don't transfer cleanly to MHC. The table below is the shortest version of the comparison.

Dimension Sword Health Withings Google Health
Surface Warm cream Cool lavender-gray White + Material pastels
Accent Single orange Single indigo Full Google rainbow, softened
Imagery Documentary worker portraits Editorial flat illustration only Aspirational athleisure photography
Voice Mission-aspirational Clinical-quiet Coach-conversational
