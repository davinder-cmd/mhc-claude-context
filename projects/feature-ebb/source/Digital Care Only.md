# Background: {#background:}

* This version of Mobile Health is in addition to our current models. We will retain 3 separate versions of the product; DCP only, Wellbeing only and Wellbeing \+ DCP.

# Goals {#goals}

* To align the Mobile Health experience with the engagement-based billing model  
  * Driving engagement with and optimizing completion of DCP programs   
  * Aligning rewards and billing with completed DCP programs while maintaining flexibility for future expansion

## 1\. Interest Survey {#1.-interest-survey}

* Purpose: personalization  
* See related docs:  
  * [Interest Survey Requirements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1w6HyhkzYO3WA-9vM8pXRfMrfpiJDdF70Dot2gDgKA4M/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.q3ar3qxpkmis)

#### Personalization {#personalization}

Optimized dynamic personalization would include:

* Health conditions/risks/likes clearly reflected in recs  
* Behavior clearly reflected in recs  
  * Programs/pages that users visit frequently are easy to access  
  * Programs/pages user clearly avoids do not continue to be pushed  
* Future: Ability to communicate dislikes/no longer interested etc.

###### *Open Questions* {#open-questions}

* How do we obtain a user’s health conditions/risks  
  * Do we need a Health Assessment and/or Health Risks?  
* How do we weight interests vs conditions/risks vs behavior vs health data (tracker)?  
  * Do we need this for v1 with so few items?

## 2\. Home Page  {#2.-home-page}

* Simplified layout   
  * Reduce distractions not related to DCP  
  * focus on:  
    * Active program(s)  
    * Next recommended session  
    * Progress and completion status  
  * Must also include access to:   
    * Rewards  
    * Messages  
    * Health Coach (tracker home)/linking a tracker  
    * Access to account info (personal information)

## 3\. DCP List Page {#3.-dcp-list-page}

Current behavior: programs are categorized into:

* In progress  
* Discover  
* Paused  
* Completed

##### Open questions: {#open-questions:}

* Will organization on the DCP list page remain the same?  
* Throttling: Do we want 2 or 3 sessions completed per week to be the default?
