# **1.  Why This Playbook Exists**  {#1. -why-this-playbook-exists}

Mobile Health has a new way to get paid. This document gets the whole team on the same page on what Engagement-Based Billing (EBB) is, how the money flows, who owns each step, the operational mechanics of billing through the claims channel, and how we launch it cleanly with our first partner, Alight. 

Read this before you sell EBB, configure a client, code or submit a claim, or build a report against it. If anything here conflicts with a signed client agreement or the Alight amendment, the executed contract wins — flag the conflict to the deal owner. 

**The one-sentence version** 

Instead of charging a flat per-employee subscription, EBB lets an employer pay out of their self-funded claims fund — and we bill $47.50 each time an eligible member completes a DCP Session inside an AI-Guided Digital Care Path, submitted through a clearinghouse to the employer's payer. 

# **2.  What Is Changing — and What Is Not**  {#2. -what-is-changing-—-and-what-is-not}

Today we bill two ways. EBB adds a third. It does not replace the others; it is a new option we lead with where the economics and the client are a fit. Under the Alight amendment, clients may elect either the traditional subscription model or the Engagement-Based Model. 

**2.1  Our three billing models** 

**Model How we bill Who pays When we get paid** 

| PEPM (Per Employee Per Month) | Flat rate per eligible employee; family members 18+ use the platform at no extra charge | Employer (benefits  budget) | Monthly, regardless of usage |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| PMPM (Per Member Per Month) | Flat rate for all eligible employees and dependents 18+ | Employer (benefits  budget) | Monthly, regardless of usage |
| **Engagement-Based  Billing (NEW)** | $47.50 per completed Session within a Digital Care Path, plus $2.50 PEPM Platform Access (waivable) | Employer's self-funded claims fund (via claims channel) | Per completed Session, as engagement happens |

**Why this matters commercially** 

PEPM/PMPM is a line item in the benefits budget — a cost the buyer scrutinizes every year. EBB is paid from the claims fund as preventative care, ties our revenue directly to outcomes, and removes the per-seat ceiling. Our Alight book-of-business modeling shows roughly a **5.6× revenue uplift** versus the PEPM model on the same population at full conversion. 

**2.2  Key terms — use these consistently** 

**Term Definition** 

| Digital Care Path (DCP)  | An AI-guided program for a specific condition. The full program is 8–10 Digital Care Sessions. |
| ----- | :---- |
| Session  | A unit of a Digital Care Path (typically 8 \- 12 per DCP \- 40 for pregnancy). Equates to roughly one week of content. An *engaged* session is the billable event ($47.50). |
| Lesson | An individual step within a DCP Session. Each DCP Session typically has about 3 \- 12 lessons. |
| Engaged session  | The billable trigger: an eligible member actively engages the session within the billing period. We bill per completed Session — not for log-ins. |
| DCP Eligible member  | An employee or dependent (18+) confirmed eligible to use a DCP.  |
| EBB Incentive Eligible member | An employee or dependent (18+) confirmed eligible by the employer/plan that can receive an EBB incentive. (eg $100 for completing a DCP topic) We bill ONLY for EBB eligible members. |
| Custom System Network (CSN)  | The per-payer billing setup configured with the employer's health plan / claims payer so our claims process and pay correctly. |
| First-dollar coverage  | Preventative/wellness positioning that makes these programs 100% covered by the employer with no member cost-share, billed to the claims fund. |
| Net Claims Revenue  | Gross per-Session fees collected via claims, *less* the $100 member incentive for the corresponding completion, *less* refunds, chargebacks, or reversals. This is the base for Alight's 20% share. |
| Clearinghouse  | The intermediary "scrubber" (Waystar) that sits between us as provider and the payers, automating claim submission, remits, and rejections. |
| Remit / eRA  | Electronic remittance advice — the payer's response showing what was paid vs. billed. Required to reconcile claims. |
| BUCA  | Bottoms-Up Claim Accounting — the master record (spreadsheet first, database later) of every claim submitted and reconciled against remits. |

# **4.  How the Money Flows**  {#4. -how-the-money-flows}

This is the core of EBB. Everyone selling or operating it should be able to draw this on a whiteboard. 

1. Member enrolls in a Digital Care Path (ex. Weight Management). The path is positioned as preventative care, eligible for first-dollar coverage.   
2. Member completes a session (\~one week of the path). This is the billable event.   
3. Mobile Health captures the engagement data and submits a claim (CPT \+ ICD-10 \+ place of service) through the Waystar clearinghouse.   
4. Waystar routes the claim to the employer's claims administrator / payer over the pre-configured Custom System Network.   
5. The payer pays $47.50 per completed Session out of the employer's self-funded claims fund; we reconcile against the electronic remit (eRA).   
6. On verified completion of the full DCP topic (all sessions within the DCP topic completed), MHC delivers a $100 incentive directly to the member (MHC-funded).   
7. Monthly, in arrears, we pay Alight 20% of Net Claims Revenue as claims are collected. 

**4.1  Unit economics per completed session**

**Item Amount Notes** 

| Billed to employer per engaged session  | $47.50  | Paid from the self-funded claims fund via claims |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Platform Access (if not waived)  | $2.50 / eligible member /  month | Waivable on the three conditions in §3.2 |
| Alight revenue share  | 20% of Net Claims Revenue  | Monthly in arrears; net of incentive & reversals |
| Member incentive (full-program completion)  | $100  | MHC-funded; subtracted before the rev-share calc |
| Program length  | 3 \- 10 lessons within 8-10 sessions  | Each session of topic billable (up to 1 session per week) |

**Cash-flow rule — protect our working capital** 

Rewards follow cash. If a member's claims have not been reimbursed, no reward is paid until we are paid. Build incentive payout to trigger off collected claims, not off engagement alone.

# **7.  Claims Billing — How It Actually Works**  {#7. -claims-billing-—-how-it-actually-works}

EBB rides on standard medical-claims infrastructure. To bill, we operate as a provider: we have a TIN and NPI, we put procedure and diagnosis codes on each claim, we submit through a clearinghouse, and we reconcile what we collect. 

**7.1  The building blocks** 

**Element What it is / our status** 

| TIN (Tax ID)  | Uses Mobile Health Inc.'s TIN. Complete |
| :---- | :---- |
| NPI (National Provider  Identifier) | Required to submit claims. **Complete** |
| CPT codes (procedure)  | 3–4 candidate codes aligned to digital care pathways; finalized with a backup. Can change later — kept out of the contract. **Complete** |
| ICD-10 / DX codes  (diagnosis) | Full diagnosis list per path, set with clinical teams. **Complete** |
| Place of service  | Remote / telehealth place of service on every claim. **Complete** |
| Clearinghouse  | Waystar — the "scrubber" between provider and payers. **In progress** |
| RTE (real-time eligibility)  | Eligibility files initially; move to RTE (e.g. Availity) later. **In progress** |
| Implementation (IMPL)  document | The packet shared with payers to set up the CSN — all CPT, DX, place-of-service, eRA & EFT details. **Complete** |
| First-dollar-coverage  memo | One-pager establishing services as wellness/preventative → 100% employer-covered, billed to the claims fund. **Complete** |

**7.2  The claim lifecycle** 

1\.  **Capture** member engagement data from the product database into a claim record. 

2\.  **Code & scrub** the claim (CPT, ICD-10, place of service) and submit a batch into Waystar.

 3\.  **Route** to the payer over their Custom System Network. 

4\.  **Reconcile** against the electronic remit (eRA): paid, partially paid, denied, rejected. 

5\.  **Work denials** and follow up with the payer to collect the balance. 

6\.  **Record everything** in BUCA and report cash received per payer and per client. 

**Two hard operational realities** 

**(1)** Most initial claims for a new payer process incorrectly — always send a small *trial batch* of 2–3 claims first to test payer readiness before releasing the backlog. **(2)** eRA (remit) enrollment can take up to 60 days per payer; enroll early or you cannot reconcile paid vs. unpaid, and request EFT (non-check) enrollment in the IMPL send-off.

# **9.  Build vs. Buy — The RCM Function**  {#9. -build-vs.-buy-—-the-rcm-function}

A key decision is whether to run revenue-cycle management (RCM) in-house or outsource it to an RCM vendor. We launch manually in-house to learn the mechanics, then scale; the build-vs-buy decision affects economics and go-live speed. 

**Dimension Build (in-house RCM) Buy (outsource)** 

| Up-front cost  | High — tech, staffing, training, process  | Medium — IMPL \+ ongoing service fees |
| ----- | ----- | :---- |
| Long-term cost  | Low if scaled efficiently with product/eng  | High — ongoing fees rise with volume |
| Time to go-live  | Long — 4–5 months  | Short — 4–6 weeks |
| Internal resourcing  | High — hire/train/manage across Tech & RCM  | Low–medium — vendor oversight |
| Tech flexibility  | High — customize, launch new pathways freely  | Low–medium — limited to vendor roadmap |
| Data ownership  | Full control of data, reporting, access  | Shared / limited by contract |
| Scalability  | High with planning; full centralized control  | Dependent on vendor capacity |
| Compliance & risk  | Internal HIPAA/billing/audit responsibility  | Vendor responsible (liability still shared) |
| RCM expertise  | Low initially — slow to build internally  | High — experienced coders & analysts |
| Adaptability to our model  | High — easy to adapt pricing & structure  | Low — locked to vendor capability |

*Conversations initiated with at least one outsourced-RCM vendor (Candid Health) for comparison. The build path ultimately supports our long-term, engagement-based model and full control, at the cost of a longer ramp.*

# **11.  Eligibility & Waived-Coverage Rules**  {#11. -eligibility-&-waived-coverage-rules}

Because EBB is billed to the medical claims fund, access is tied to medical coverage. These rules are non negotiable and protect the first-dollar-coverage position.   
**Coverage-gated access** 

* **DCPs and the platform are NOT available to employees who have waived medical coverage.**  
* If an employer pays PEPM \+ DCP via EBB, **waived-coverage employees do not get DCPs or incentives.**   
* **No reward is paid until the corresponding claims are reimbursed** — rewards follow collected cash, not engagement.   
* If an employer pays PEPM for both Wellbeing AND DCP, AND pay PEPM for Employees who waive Medical coverage they can have access to DCP but NOT the $100 Incentive funded by MH from Claims fund reimbursement.

Open item for the team to finalize: how access works when an employee does not enroll in medical coverage at all — confirm the eligibility-file logic before go-live so ineligible lives are never billed. 

# **12.  Reporting & Compliance Requirements**  {#12. -reporting-&-compliance-requirements}

EBB only works if we can prove what we billed and why. Reporting is how we defend first-dollar coverage, protect the Alight partnership, and manage cash. Every EBB client needs reporting that demonstrates: 

1\.  **Eligibility integrity** — that ONLY eligible employees and dependents (18+, not waived) were billed. 

2\.  **Billing transparency** — how much was billed, against which completed Session, per payer and per client.

3\.  **Clinical impact** — pre- and post-clinical assessment change for members who completed a program (also feeds Alight's user-profile build to suggest further interventions). 

**12.1  Claim-health KPIs to track** 

**KPI Why it matters** 

| Denials rate  | Signals coding/payer-setup problems eroding collections |
| :---- | :---- |
| Time to cash  | How fast engagement converts to collected revenue |
| Time to bill / charge lag  | Delay between engagement and claim submission |
| Net days in A/R  | Working-capital health of the claims book |

Start KPIs manually at the payer and client level, then move to a dynamic Data-team database. Hold the eligibility/ Session standard on every invoice cycle: if we cannot tie a bill to an eligible member and a completed Session, we do not bill it.
