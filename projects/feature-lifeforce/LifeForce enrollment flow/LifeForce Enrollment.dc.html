LifeForce, Truist's premier well-being program, rewards you for building and maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

How it works

Meet regularly with a registered nurse coach by telehealth or phone. Your coach reviews your labs and health assessment, then sets personal goals with you. As you make progress, you earn monthly medical credit.

Who it's for

Truist medically-enrolled teammates who want steady, one-on-one coaching toward their personal health goals.

Your first Peak Health nurse appointment is typically scheduled within ~20 business days after all steps are complete.
