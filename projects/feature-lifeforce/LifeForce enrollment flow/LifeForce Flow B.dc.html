About LifeForce
Truist · Special Program
LifeForce

LifeForce, Truist's premier well-being program, rewards you for building and maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

How it works

Meet regularly with a registered nurse coach by telehealth or phone. Your coach reviews your labs and health assessment, then sets personal goals with you. As you progress through the five LifeForce phases, you earn monthly medical credit toward your plan.

Who it's for

Truist medically-enrolled teammates who want steady, one-on-one coaching toward their personal health goals.

Your phase reflects where you are today. Keep meeting with your nurse and working your goals to move toward Phase 5 and a fuller medical credit.
