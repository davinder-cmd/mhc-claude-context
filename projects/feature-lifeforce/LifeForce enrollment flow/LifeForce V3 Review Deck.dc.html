01
User flow
First-time enrollment
What an unenrolled teammate sees, and the moment they join.
First-time · Unenrolled
About LifeForce
The full program overview shown to teammates who haven't enrolled. Explains how it works, who it's for, and earning potential. Primary action: Enroll & earn 200 points.
First-time · Enrolling
Complete enrollment
Right after enrolling, the two-tab home appears. CarePlus is marked done; the remaining three tasks — Program Acknowledgment, lab work, and Health Assessment — sit in Next steps.
02
User flow
Status tab — enrolled
How the primary screen adapts from first appointment through recurring tasks and dependents.
Status · Enrolled
First appointment being scheduled
The waiting state before the first nurse visit. The LifeForce Phase card is intentionally hidden until the first appointment is scheduled.
Status · Enrolled
First visit complete
An Active confirmation replaces the appointment card. Ongoing nurse guidance moves under the My Progress tab.
Status · Recurring
Health Assessment due
Recurring tasks reuse the same Next-steps pattern. The Health Assessment reappears with its due date (shown: Due June 30).
Status · Dependents
Dependent reminder
The Spouse / Domestic Partner has registered but not finished their Health Assessment — shown in the Maximize your credit box below the appointment.
Status · Dependents
Dependent complete
Once the dependent's Health Assessment is done, the box confirms the credit is maximized and the prompt clears.
03
User flow
My Progress tab
Labs, nurse guidance, programs, and handouts — with persistent zero states.
My Progress · Unenrolled
Locked zero state
Teammates who haven't enrolled still see the tab, with a single clear prompt to enroll before any progress exists.
My Progress · Active
Populated progress
Priority order: labs → nurse guidance → programs → handouts. Everything an active member needs between visits.
My Progress · Empty
Empty states
Enrolled but nothing to show yet — each section holds a solid-bordered zero state instead of collapsing.
