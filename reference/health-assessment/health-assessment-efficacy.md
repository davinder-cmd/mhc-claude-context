# Mobile Health Consumer — Health Assessment Efficacy

## Disease Risks: Criteria and Sources

Predictive risk scores derived from the health assessment, grouped by demographic. Each condition lists its clinical source, risk timeframe, and the assessment criteria (inputs) used to compute the score.

---

## MEN

### Prostate Cancer
- **Source:** National Cancer Institute; US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF)
- **Risk timeframe:** Lifetime risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Which of the following do you consider your race/ethnicity?
  - The last time you had your Prostate Specific Antigen (PSA) checked, what was it? (ng/mL)
  - The last time you had a digital rectal exam (DRE), did your doctor inform you it was enlarged or concerning for prostate cancer?
  - Have you ever had a prostate biopsy?

### Colorectal Cancer
- **Source:** National Cancer Institute; CDC; USPSTF
- **Risk timeframe:** 5-year risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Weight / height (BMI)
  - Which of the following do you consider your race/ethnicity?
  - On average, how many hours a week do you spend doing vigorous exercise?
  - On most days, how many cups of vegetables do you eat?
  - How many years have you smoked?
  - How many packs of cigarettes do you smoke per day?
  - Have you had a sigmoidoscopy or colonoscopy in the past 10 years?
  - In the past 30 days, have you taken medications containing aspirin at least 3 times per week (includes Bufferin, Bayer, Excedrin, or other generic forms)?

### Chronic Lung Disease
- **Source:** Martinez FJ, Raczek AE, Seifer FD, et al. Development and initial validation of a self-scored COPD Population Screener Questionnaire (COPD-PS). COPD. 2008;5(2):85–95; USPSTF
- **Risk timeframe:** Lifetime risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Have you smoked at least 100 cigarettes in your ENTIRE LIFE?
  - During the past 4 weeks, how often do you feel short of breath?
  - Do you ever cough up mucous or phlegm?
  - Do you feel you are less active in the past 12 months due to breathing problems?

### Diabetes
- **Source:** Framingham Study; American Diabetes Association; CDC; USPSTF
- **Risk timeframe:** 8-year risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Weight / height (BMI)
  - Blood pressure (systolic / diastolic)
  - Cholesterol (HDL / LDL)
  - Triglyceride level
  - Fasting glucose (mg/dL)

### Congestive Heart Failure
- **Source:** Framingham Study
- **Risk timeframe:** 4-year risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Blood pressure (systolic / diastolic)
  - Resting heart rate (bpm)
  - Have you been diagnosed with diabetes?
  - Have you been diagnosed with coronary artery disease?
  - Have you been diagnosed with heart valve disease?
  - Has your doctor told you that you have left ventricular hypertrophy shown on your ECG?

### Coronary Artery Disease
- **Source:** Framingham Study; CDC; American College of Cardiology
- **Risk timeframe:** 10-year risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Blood pressure (systolic / diastolic)
  - Cholesterol (HDL / LDL)
  - Do you currently smoke cigarettes?
  - Have you been diagnosed with diabetes?

### Stroke

## WOMEN

### Breast Cancer
- **Source:** National Cancer Institute; The Gail Model
- **Risk timeframe:** 5-year risk
- **Criteria:**
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Which of the following do you consider your race/ethnicity?
  - How old were you when you had your first period?
  - How old were you when you had your first live birth of a child?
  - Have you ever had a breast biopsy?
  - Do you have a history of breast cancer?
