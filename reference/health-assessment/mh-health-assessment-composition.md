## Overview

Mobile Health's NCQA-certified assessment collects important attributes about the individual's health and wellbeing, which in turn triggers health coaching, education, programs, tools, and guidance to eligible health resources. The assessment consists of about 50 biometric, health, lifestyle, and gender-related questions should the individual choose to report gender. The health assessment establishes a baseline for individuals to better understand their health, wellbeing, and health risks which are derived from the assessment's built-in predictive analytics. Clients can add custom questions to the assessment and choose to import employee biometric data from their preferred biometric vendor.

The assessment takes about five minutes to complete, easily allowing a person to tap through the answers on a mobile device or access via a computer. Mobile Health's health assessment is proprietary and was developed under the supervision of our Chief Medical Officer Dr. Christian J. Fox and the Medical Team at the University of California Irvine School of Medicine. The built-in predictive analytics leverage the most widely-accepted clinical trials such as the Framingham Heart Study. Clients can choose to add custom questions to the assessment or to import employee biometric data from their preferred biometric vendor.

Once completed, individuals receive confirmation of completion along with health coaching based on risks reported and risks proactively identified using predictive analytics. Personalization of the platform then promotes programs and services available both native to the platform and additional external resources as available.
