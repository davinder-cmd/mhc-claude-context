# Brand Voice Guide — Member-Facing

> Voice for anything a member reads **inside the product**: onboarding, notifications, program content, error states, celebration moments, UX microcopy. Distinct from `Brand Voice Guide - Stakeholder & Partner.md` (talking *about* members to executives, payers, health systems) and `Brand Voice Guide - Product & Design.md` (talking to your own team). "Members" here means the end user — see `strategy/Ideal Customer Profile.md`.

---

## Voice in One Line

Plain, warm, and direct — a member should never have to re-read a sentence to know what to do next.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

Members are not in an exploratory mindset. They're often stressed, in pain, or managing a condition they didn't choose, reading this on a lunch break, in a truck cab, or at 3am. Simplicity isn't a style choice — it's a responsibility. Every word either helps them take the next step or it's in the way.

---

## Tone Principles

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Plain language** | No medical jargon, no internal product terms ("DCP," "EBB," "Page Layout"). Say what a person would say. |
| **One idea per message** | One takeaway, one action — never a data dump. If it needs two sentences to explain, cut one. |
| **Encouraging, not clinical** | Acknowledge effort and progress like a supportive coach, not a chart. Warm, never saccharine. |
| **Concrete over abstract** | "3 of 4 sessions done" beats "You're making great progress on your journey." |
| **Never assume health literacy** | Write for the person avoiding medical terms, not the person who already knows them. |

---

## Reading Level & Mechanics

- Target a 6th–8th grade reading level
- Short sentences, active voice
- Numbers over adjectives ("3 sessions left," not "almost there")
- Earn warmth through clarity, not exclamation points

---

## Examples

**Good:** "Session 3 of 4 done. One more to unlock your $100."
**Avoid:** "You're crushing it! Keep up the amazing momentum on your wellness journey!"

**Good:** "Your blood pressure reading was above your goal range."
**Avoid:** "Your BP reading indicates a value outside optimal parameters."

**Good:** "Text your question anytime — a health expert will respond."
**Avoid:** "Leverage our AI-powered conversational health assistant for real-time support."

---

## Terminology

Address the person as **"you,"** never "the member" — third person is for talking about them to stakeholders, not to them directly.
